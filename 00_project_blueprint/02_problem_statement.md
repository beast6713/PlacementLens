# Problem Statement

Placement-related student data is underused for understanding outcome patterns, preparation gaps, and training priorities. Students and placement teams need a trustworthy view of how academic performance, technical skills, assessments, internships, and projects are **associated** with recorded placement outcomes.

PlacementLens will provide descriptive, comparative, and association-focused analytics. It will not determine why an individual is placed, prove that an intervention caused an outcome, or make hiring decisions.

## Intended decisions supported

- Which aggregated preparation areas warrant placement-cell training attention.
- How students can compare readiness indicators with project-defined benchmarks.
- Which branches or cohorts merit further investigation, not preferential treatment.
