# Business Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Provide an overall placement-rate view. | Validated metric in Python, SQL, and Power BI. |
| BR-02 | Compare placement outcomes by branch, CGPA band, skills, experience, and assessments. | Reproducible tables/visuals addressing AQ-002–AQ-011. |
| BR-03 | Describe package distribution for placed students. | Average, median, maximum, distribution, and branch comparison. |
| BR-04 | Identify aggregate preparation gaps and priorities. | Evidence-backed insight document with limitations. |
| BR-05 | Provide an explainable readiness framework. | Published formula, ranges, and component values. |
| BR-06 | Preserve reproducibility and handoff continuity. | Checkpoints, validation records, change log, and ordered assets. |
| BR-07 | Protect privacy and avoid harmful claims. | Synthetic/de-identified data, ethics notice, and no causal/predictive claims. |
