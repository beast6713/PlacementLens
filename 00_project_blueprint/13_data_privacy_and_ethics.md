# Data Privacy and Ethics

## Privacy

The dataset is synthetic. It must contain no real names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, roll numbers, government identifiers, institutional identifiers, private academic records, or any linkage key. `student_id` is synthetic and non-identifying.

## Appropriate use

- Use only to demonstrate analytics and inform aggregate preparation discussions.
- Treat observed relationships as associations, not causes.
- Do not use the readiness score as a hiring, screening, ranking-for-access, or admissions decision.
- Do not infer protected characteristics or profile individuals beyond transparent, project-defined preparation indicators.

## Fairness and communication

Always show counts alongside rates where feasible; interpret sparse segments cautiously. Gender is included only for optional aggregate dashboard filtering and is not a readiness-index input or recommended decision factor. Document data limitations, synthetic-data status, and methodology in every final presentation.
