# Insight Framework

Each final finding uses this mandatory template:

1. **Question:** the linked analytical question.
2. **Metric:** calculation, population, filters, and denominator.
3. **Observation:** concise factual result from completed analysis.
4. **Data evidence:** notebook cell/output, SQL query, and/or dashboard visual reference.
5. **Interpretation:** association-focused business meaning.
6. **Limitation:** synthetic-data, sample, confounding, or measurement limitation.
7. **Recommendation:** proportionate action to investigate, train, or support.

Only 5–8 major findings will be promoted to the final narrative. No result may be drafted before analysis, and no language may claim a factor caused placement or guarantees it.
